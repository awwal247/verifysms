How To

Short videos that show you exactly how VerifySMS works. Watch once, then do it yourself.

Funding your wallet
You need money in your wallet before you can buy anything. This walkthrough takes you from the dashboard to a topped-up balance.
  1. Open Top Up from the menu.
  2. Enter an amount — anything from {{ min_topup|default:"100" }} to {{ max_topup|default:"500000" }}. The fee and the exact amount that reaches your wallet are shown before you pay.
  3. Press Pay. You are taken to Paystack, our payment provider, to complete the payment.
  4. Pay with your card or a bank transfer, whichever is easier for you.
  5. You come back to your dashboard with the new balance already there.

Buying a number
Pick a service, pick a country, buy the number. The whole thing takes under a minute once your wallet has money in it.
  1. Open Services. Everything you can buy is listed there with the live price.
  2. Pick the service you need the code for — WhatsApp, Telegram, Google, and so on. Read the descriptions: some services have their own rules about how long the number stays yours.
  3. Pick the country of the number you want.
  4. Pick an operator. Only operators with numbers in stock are shown, so anything you can click is available right now.
  5. Check the summary — the price, and what your wallet balance will be after. Have a coupon? Type it in and press Apply before you buy.
  6. Press Buy Number. The price comes straight out of your wallet, and the number appears under Active.
  7. Open that order and your phone number is at the top — enter it in the app you're verifying. When the code lands it shows up in the same order, usually within a minute. It stays there, and History keeps it after the order ends.
2 min 32 sec Paid from your wallet balance No SIM, no personal number

Good to know
Keep your wallet funded first. Numbers are paid for from your balance the moment you order, so top up before you start.

Know the app you're verifying. Pick the service you need the code for — WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, and so on. The number works for that service only.

The code arrives in the app. Open the order in Active and the message appears there as soon as it lands, usually within a minute. The page can be closed and reopened, and History keeps every past order with a "View SMS" link.

Something not working? The Customer Care button reaches support on WhatsApp.
